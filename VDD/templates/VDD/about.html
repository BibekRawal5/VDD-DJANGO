About

What is the Leaf Condition Classifier?

The Leaf Condition Classifier is a tool designed to help identify various conditions affecting fruits and vegetables by analyzing images of their leaves. Using advanced machine learning algorithms, the classifier can detect diseases and offer guidance for better crop management.

Classification Types

The system currently supports classification for the following types:

  • Pepper (Bell):
    • Bacterial Spot
    • Healthy
  • Potato:
    • Early Blight
    • Late Blight
    • Healthy
  • Tomato:
    • Bacterial Spot
    • Early Blight
    • Late Blight
    • Leaf Mold
    • Septoria Leaf Spot
    • Spider Mites (Two-Spotted Spider Mite)
    • Target Spot
    • Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus
    • Tomato Mosaic Virus
    • Healthy

How It Works

Upload an image of a leaf, and the classifier will analyze it to identify any potential conditions. This system uses a pre-trained neural network model to deliver accurate results. Farmers, gardeners, and agricultural experts can use this tool to make informed decisions about plant health and care.
